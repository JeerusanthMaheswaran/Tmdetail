FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How long does a detail take?

Express takes about 1.5 hours, a Full Detail around 4 hours, and ceramic coating packages usually take 1–2 days.

Do you come to me?

Yes — we're fully mobile and come to your home or workplace. Just send your address when you book.

Do I need to provide water or power?

An outdoor tap and power outlet help. If you don't have them, tell us when booking and we'll sort it out.

How do I pay?

You pay when the job is finished and you're happy. We accept cash, card and e-transfer.

How long does ceramic coating last?

Depending on the coating and how the car is maintained, from 2 to 5 years.

What if it rains?

We keep an eye on the forecast and will message you on WhatsApp to reschedule for free if the weather won't cooperate.
