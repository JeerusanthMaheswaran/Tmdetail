What We Do

Detailing services

Every service is done by hand with professional-grade products that are safe for your paint, trim and interior.

Exterior Detail

Hand wash, wheel and tire clean, clay bar decontamination and a protective sealant for a deep, glossy finish.

Interior Detail

Thorough vacuum, steam clean, shampoo of carpets and seats, leather conditioning and streak-free glass.

Paint Correction

Machine polishing to remove swirl marks, light scratches and oxidation, restoring clarity and depth to your paint.

Ceramic Coating

Long-lasting protection against UV, road salt and grime, with incredible water beading and easier washes.

Headlight Restoration

Remove yellowing and haze from headlights for a cleaner look and better night-time visibility.

Engine Bay Clean

Safe degreasing and dressing of the engine bay so it looks as good under the hood as it does outside.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does a detail take?

An Express detail takes about 1–2 hours, a Full Detail 3–5 hours, and ceramic coating packages usually take a full day.

Do you come to me?

Yes — we offer mobile detailing at your home or workplace. Just let us know your address when you book.

Do I need to provide water or power?

Access to an outdoor tap and power outlet is helpful. If that isn't available, mention it when booking and we'll work something out.

How do I pay?

Payment is taken once the job is finished and you're happy with the result. We accept cash, card and e-transfer.

How long does ceramic coating last?

Depending on the coating chosen and how the car is maintained, ceramic coatings last from 2 to 5 years.
